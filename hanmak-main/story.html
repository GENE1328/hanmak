THE BEGINNING2000s / KOKOPO, EAST NEW BRITAIN

HANMAK Apparel started with a simple vision in Malakuna No. 4 Village, Kokopo, East New Britain: to create fresh, affordable streetwear that reflects real Papua New Guinean identity.

Not an imported mood. Not a borrowed uniform. Something that starts close to home and still feels good wherever it travels.

“

What began as a grassroots printing operation grew through word-of-mouth, market pop-ups and community support.

A label grows in public

Every conversation, market day and shared recommendation built the next step. HANMAK grew because people made room for it: friends, customers, communities and the young resellers who saw a way to carry something local.

The work is still grounded in that exchange. A shirt can be a product, but it can also be a small piece of belonging.
